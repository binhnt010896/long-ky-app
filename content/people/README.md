# Photo drop folders — standalone people (Cycle R onward)

Each figure who stands alone (on no era roster) has a folder here,
`content/people/<id>/`. Their app images will live in the same folder
(`avatar.png` 1:1 and `full.png` 2:3). Standalone events follow the same pattern
under `content/events/<id>/`.

## How to drop a photo

1. Put the photo in the person's folder as **`<id>.jpg`** (the file name you used; PNG or WebP is
   fine too). If you have more than one, name the rest `<id>-reference.jpg` (a second image used to restore lost detail),
   `source-3.jpg` and so on. A full-length photo is welcome next to the
   portrait.
2. Add **`credit.txt`** with where it came from: a URL, or the archive or museum
   name, plus the photographer if known. This becomes the caption and our
   rights note.
3. **🔍 names** (see the lists below):
   - a real photo → `source.jpg` as above;
   - only a traditional painted / altar portrait → **`altar.jpg`**;
   - nothing exists → an empty **`none.txt`** (Higgsfield then generates the
     portrait from the bio).

**The years below are only working labels** to help you search. They are
checked against sources when each bio is written.

**Good enough means:** the largest copy you can find (≥ 800 px on the short
side), a frontal or three-quarter face, and no watermark across the face.

**Storage:** image files here are git-ignored, like all content media. They
reach the private sources bucket with `tool/push_sources.sh`. The `.txt` files
*are* tracked. Empty folders exist only on this Mac.

**If you can't find a photo:** never mind. The person ships with a held
portrait (a lacquer tile, no face) and their street still lights up. We never
invent a face for someone who was photographed.
