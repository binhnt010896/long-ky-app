## What the review found in the current text

- The current `cau-vien-ba-da-loc` event already criticises the cầu viện. The
  hero tone sits mainly in the **Gia Long bio and epithet**, the **era
  overview**, the **Bá Đa Lộc bio** and the **"thống nhất / thu giang sơn"
  wording**.
- Two factual problems, to fix regardless: that event says the Siamese sent
  **hai vạn** troops; *Nhân Dân* says **5 vạn** [S2, S3]. And "Pháp bội ước"
  is stated as fact with no trusted source ⚠ (see the first open question).
- The Tây Sơn events' text is already correct; only their **citation** (the
  Nguyễn court's *Ngụy Tây liệt truyện*) changes.

## Source and wording rules used throughout

- "Lập nên / lên ngôi / cai quản", never "khai sáng / thống nhất giang sơn /
  thu về một mối" for Nguyễn Ánh.
- The unification is credited to the Tây Sơn [S3, S7, S8].
- The HCM lines are quoted with attribution, never as the app's own voice [S1].
- Folk detail (the "Gia Long = Gia Định + Thăng Long" etymology) stays,
  labelled "tương truyền".
