# Street-fill images — what to prepare (Cycle R and later waves)

Companion to `EXECUTION.md` (Cycle R). One row per image job. Nothing here has
been generated or collected yet.

**Legend**
- 📷 **You provide a real photo.** The person or event was photographed. We do
  **not** invent a face (photo-fidelity rule). You drop the photo in the repo; we
  restore it, colourise it if it is black-and-white, and extend it to the two app
  shapes in Higgsfield, checking each face against the original.
- 🔍 **Check first.** Mid/late 19th century: a photograph *may* exist. Please
  look (VNA/TTXVN, Bảo tàng Lịch sử Quốc gia, the provincial museum, the đền thờ).
  - If you find a **photo**, treat the row as 📷.
  - If only a **traditional painted portrait** exists (the one on the đền thờ
    altar, say), send it. We use it as the Higgsfield likeness reference.
  - If **nothing** exists, treat the row as 🎨.
  - Until then, the person ships with a **held portrait** (a lacquer tile, no
    face). Their street still lights up.
- 🎨 **Higgsfield generates it** from the brief: `nano_banana_pro`, the
  painterly-realism sơn mài style lock, full-bleed (no frame or panel), no text,
  letters, signatures or Hán tự, and no map of Vietnam. Each brief is
  re-checked against the person's bio and source (ĐVSKTT, or the source we
  cite) before generating.

**Where the files go:** every new person is *standalone*, on no era roster
(decisions R1 and R2). Their images live in `content/people/<id>/`:
`avatar.png` (1:1 head-and-shoulders), `full.png` (2:3 standing), plus your
source photo as `source.jpg` and its credit in `credit.txt`. A standalone event's
hero is `content/events/<id>/hero.png`. **The 📷 / 🔍 lists, as bullets, and
the drop instructions are in `content/people/README.md`; the folders are
already created.** The rest of this file holds the Higgsfield briefs.

A head-only photo is fine: we extend it to full body by keeping the face and
inventing only period-correct clothing and background.
