## G. Accuracy checks on the pasted article

- **Phan Bá Vành's rising was under Minh Mạng, not Gia Long.** It is dated
  1821–27 / 1825–27, and he was executed in 1827 (Wikipedia and general
  sources; still needs a trusted source). Gia Long died in 1820. → **Leave it
  out** of anything about Gia Long's reign.
- **"Bế quan tỏa cảng"**: no trusted source attributes it to Gia Long. S6 says
  the opposite: relations with France were kept up through his reign.
  → **Leave it out.**
- **"Cao Bá Chúc", "Nguyễn Văn Nhàn, Lê Văn Bột"**: not found in any trusted
  source. → **Leave them out.**
- **Rạch Gầm date:** S2 says the night of 19–20/1/1785. The current
  `rach-gam-xoai-mut` event should match.
- **Versailles date:** VN sources say **28/11/1787**; some foreign sources say
  21/11. Use 28/11 (S4).

## Decision needed

**D1. The positive record in official sources.** S11 (*Nhân Dân*, 2014) and the
Huế heritage pages credit Gia Long with Hoàng Sa / Trường Sa sovereignty acts
and the full S-shaped territory. Vietnam's sovereignty case relies on those
acts. Proposal: keep **one neutral, dated sentence** about the Hoàng Sa
sovereignty acts (it is a state position, not praise), and keep the
condemnation of the cầu viện as the bio's frame.
